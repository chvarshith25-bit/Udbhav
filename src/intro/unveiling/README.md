# The Grand Unveiling artwork

The bronze starting gates and the two views of the shared arena are original generated concept art. They illustrate a festival atmosphere and do not depict a verified venue or participants.

The landscape and portrait compositions each ship in two WebP widths. The browser requests one arena image according to orientation and resolution. Both gate leaves share one texture. The Latin Barlow Condensed font is bundled under the SIL Open Font License included here.
